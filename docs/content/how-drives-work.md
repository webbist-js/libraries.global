**What a drive is.** A drive is a short, shared push to fill one kind of gap across a set of libraries: opening hours for Ohio's public libraries, say, or photos for a county's branches. Every drive has one Focus, a finish line and an end date, so it can be finished.

**How to help.** Open a drive from the Noticeboard. Pick a library from the list, or press "Give me a library". We keep it for you for 20 minutes so nobody else works on it. Fill the gap in the small panel beside the list, say where you found it, and send. A Moderator checks every change before it goes live, like any other correction.

**What counts.** A change counts towards a drive when it fills that drive's gap for a library in its scope, whether you sent it from the drive or from the library's own page. Each accepted task earns 2 points on top of the change's own. When a drive reaches its goal, everyone with 5 or more accepted tasks earns 25 more.

**Kinds of drive.** Some drives fill a gap, such as opening hours, a photo, accessibility details or contact details. Some ask you to confirm details that nobody has checked for a while. Others complete whole records, a section at a time.

**Proposing a drive.** Once 5 of your changes have been accepted, you can propose a drive from the Noticeboard, from a place's page or from a search on Find libraries. Choose the gap and the places, say where the answers can be found and on what terms, and pick the dates. A Moderator reviews it, and approved drives open on their start date. A drive can have at most 2,000 libraries to do and can run for at most 90 days.

**Sources.** Facts from a library's own website are fine. Data copied from map services isn't, because its licence doesn't allow it.
