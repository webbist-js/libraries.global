# Membership, billing and payments

Every library record, individual record download, contribution, follow and saved event remains free. The weekly library digest and community report are separate free opt-ins. A membership never changes your community role, reputation, review process or staff access.

Supporter costs £3 per month or £30 per year and adds detailed library change history and Atlas CSV/GeoJSON exports of up to 500 libraries per download. Pro costs £5 per month or £50 per year at the current founding price. It includes Supporter tools, exports of up to 10,000 libraries and delivered population comparisons. Future analysis features are labelled coming soon. Team has a separate annual subscription and organisation billing flow when enabled; existing invoice agreements are preserved. See [Team billing](TEAM-BILLING.md). Team is not part of personal checkout.

## Buying a membership

Choose a plan and billing interval on the Membership page. Sign in or create a free account, verify your email, choose recognition preferences and read the membership terms. The consent requests immediate access to the tools and does not waive statutory cancellation rights.

Stripe Managed Payments hosts the payment page. The prices above are tax-inclusive base GBP prices; Stripe confirms tax, any local currency conversion and the final recurring total before payment. Eligible payment methods, including Apple Pay or Google Pay when available, appear on Stripe's page. Libraries Global never receives your full card details or CVC.

The Libraries Global operator identified in the published terms is the seller and supplier of the membership service. Stripe Managed Payments is merchant of record for the payment transaction; this does not replace Libraries Global as the service seller. Stripe sends financial receipts and invoices. Libraries Global separately confirms membership access. Payment confirmation can take a short time while the signed notification is checked. If confirmation is delayed, check Settings rather than paying again.

## Membership and billing in Settings

The Membership & billing panel shows the paid plan, current period, founding price where applicable, masked card, billing details and recent invoice PDFs. Current access is shown separately because a contribution reward, verification, grant or Team seat can provide complimentary Pro.

Manage membership opens your owned Stripe billing page. Use it to update payment/billing information, view invoices or stop renewal. Change plan opens a selection dialog. Paid upgrades continue to Stripe's exact charge and unused-time credit review. Lower plans and shorter intervals are reviewed in Settings, with no charge today, and start when the paid period ends. Use Undo scheduled change before it starts. For an interval change made in Stripe's portal, use Update subscription → Cancel change. Changing your Stripe billing email does not change your sign-in address; access notices use your current verified account email.

A new Pro upgrade takes effect only when its invoice is settled. An unpaid upgrade cannot grant Pro or remove a still-paid Supporter period. A failed renewal can retain paid tools for a bounded seven-day grace period from the first failure. Updating the card does not itself prove payment; follow Stripe's recovery instructions and wait for confirmation.

## Exports and change history

Atlas exports contain the libraries matching the selected scope, with attribution and licensing information. Supporter allows up to 500 rows in one download; Pro 10,000 and Team 50,000. If the scope is larger, narrow it or use a higher-limit plan. The app refuses an oversized export and does not silently truncate it. CSV and GeoJSON use the same plan cap. Downloading a single library's public record remains free.

The public change timeline still shows what was changed. Supporter, Pro and Team can also inspect detailed before/after values. Payment does not give editing or moderation authority.

## Recognition and privacy

The Supporter mark and public supporters-page credit are independent optional choices in Privacy settings. Public credit requires a public profile; private and limited profiles are never listed. Checkout does not change profile visibility. Recognition ends when eligible access ends, while saved preferences remain available for a later membership.

## Cancellation, refunds and account deletion

Cancel renewal in Membership & billing, or through Link using your financial confirmation if you cannot enter the app. Settled access normally continues to the paid period end. Cancellation of future renewal is separate from a refund request. Read our cancellation/refund policy and the Link terms shown at checkout. Link’s refund policy prevails over a more restrictive policy we set, and statutory rights remain unchanged. Contact info@libraries.global for product/access help and use the transaction-support route on the Stripe confirmation for payment or refund help.

Refund and dispute decisions can suspend the refunded paid access; any independent complimentary access still applies. Deleting an account first stops its subscriptions and expires open checkout sessions. If that cannot be confirmed, deletion is refused so billing cannot continue against an erased account. Provider financial records and minimal reconciliation references can remain where needed for accounting, disputes or legal obligations.

## Troubleshooting

- Missing invoice or uncertain payment: use the same account and secure billing page. Do not share sign-in links, full card details or passwords with support.
- Apple Pay/Google Pay absent: availability depends on the device, browser, wallet and location. Use another payment method Stripe offers.
- Membership still confirming: allow time for synchronization, then contact support with the invoice reference shown in your own billing page.
- Paid tools unavailable after cancellation or failure: check the paid period, recovery status and whether another complimentary access source applies.

Essential billing/access notices do not subscribe you to marketing. Newsletter, product updates and weekly emails have their own Settings choices.
