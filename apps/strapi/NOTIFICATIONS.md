# Notifications and email

Implementation review: 11 October 2026. This describes the running implementation, including the weekly email split agreed during the review.

## In-app triggers

| Action or event                                                                    | Who sees it                             | Email behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Follow another contributor                                                         | The contributor being followed          | `newFollowers`, off by default. Self-follow is refused; unfollow has no alert; repeated follows deduplicate. Private follower identities are withheld.                                                                                                        |
| Publish or update a library's public information                                   | Accounts following that library         | Hourly inbox rollup, at minute 17 UTC; at most one update per library/day. Drafts and imports are excluded. No immediate email; followed-library updates feed the weekly digest.                                                                              |
| Approve, reject or request more information on a submission                        | Its submitter                           | `editsReviewed`, on by default. Covers corrections, new libraries, library edits, Knowledge edits, Journal submissions, topic suggestions, drive proposals and event feeds. Role application decisions and approved staff claims use essential service email. |
| Close a drive manually, at its goal or at its deadline                             | Contributors who helped                 | `drives`, on by default. Includes final totals and the recipient's contribution.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Earn a badge                                                                       | The contributor earning it              | `recognition`, off by default. Existing or duplicate awards do not notify again.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Approve a new library staff member                                                 | Existing library owners                 | Essential service email. The new member also gets the approved-claim decision.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Remove staff access or leave a library's staff                                     | The affected member                     | Essential service email.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Change a staff member's level or permission to go live                             | The affected member                     | Essential service email. Saving an unchanged value has no notification.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Replace, resume or disconnect an event feed, or automatically pause a failing feed | Other staff claimants for that library  | Essential service email. The actor is excluded. No credential details are included.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A temporary project role grant expires                                             | The grant holder                        | Essential service email. The scheduled resync considers grants expired in the preceding 48 hours; repeated runs use the same event key. Other active grants still apply.                                                                                      |
| Publish a Notification announcement in Strapi                                      | Accounts created before its publication | `editorialMessages` for project notices (on by default), explicit `marketing` consent for product updates (off by default), or `transactional` for essential security/legal/access notices. Every channel creates an inbox entry.                             |
| Generate a non-empty followed-library weekly digest                                | Accounts explicitly opted into it       | `weeklyDigest`, off by default; Monday 09:00 UTC.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Generate a non-empty weekly community report                                       | Accounts explicitly opted into it       | `weeklyReport`, off by default; Monday 09:05 UTC.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Inbox activity remains available when its optional activity email is disabled. Weekly entries are generated only for people who opted into that weekly email. Account creation, password resets, newsletter requests, beta entry, saving events, following a library, unfollowing, settings changes and read receipts do not themselves create an inbox notification. There is no submission reply thread; the existing review note is part of the decision notification.

The header has one account control. Unread activity adds a dot to the avatar and a count to its Notifications dropdown entry and the owner's profile tab. The inbox uses the profile hero and tabs with one full-width card. It groups entries into Today / This week / Earlier, with All / Unread / Contributions / Libraries / Community / News & digests filters. Delivery controls live in Settings, reached by a button in the inbox header. The empty state offers Find libraries and Contribute actions; an empty filter can be cleared. It shows the latest 50 entries; unread totals cover all retained entries. Marking entries read clears their unread state.

`soundOn` is off by default. When enabled, a new notification can play two quiet, short notes while the page is visible and after a browser gesture. Initial loads, read receipts and historical entries are silent. Settings includes a preview button. A shared provider avoids duplicated polling and chimes from desktop/mobile headers; a local-storage marker suppresses repeats across tabs. Notifications poll every 30 seconds while visible.

## Two independent weekly emails

**Weekly library digest:** public library names from the recipient's followed libraries that were published or updated during the last completed Monday–Sunday UTC week. No drafts, imported library batches or private actor information. No updates means no email. The email lists up to 100 libraries, with a link to each public record when its geographic path is complete, plus a following-list button. It does not claim detailed field diffs or immediate alerts.

**Weekly community report:** a short account-specific email containing recorded public library additions, other libraries updated, saved edit batches, accepted content contributions, new Knowledge/Journal articles, up to three reading links, and the recipient's own accepted contributions, net points and current review backlog. Other people's private notes and identities are excluded. Review notes stay in My submissions. Counts describe recorded activity: the audit history cannot establish the first-ever publication of legacy content without an earlier record. Imports are excluded from library metrics. A week with neither community activity nor the recipient's completed activity sends nothing; an unchanged pending submission alone does not generate a report.

Both require a new explicit opt-in recorded with a consent timestamp. They have independent settings and unsubscribe links. A legacy planned switch value is not consent. The monthly newsletter is a third, separate subscription; neither weekly option enrolls a person in it. `productUpdates` is a legacy stored key with no trigger; the live Product updates control uses `marketing` plus its explicit consent timestamp.

Essential access and requested sign-in messages do not use optional email settings or an unsubscribe footer. Optional queued account emails carry a visible category-specific unsubscribe link and RFC 8058 one-click headers. Confirmation and unsubscribe pages require neither sign-in nor the beta password. Opening a link is read-only; the user action/one-click POST applies the change. Guest confirmations are cancelled if consent is withdrawn before sending. Recipients and preferences are checked again at delivery time. Queued messages for deleted accounts are skipped or erased.

The old Strapi end-user activation lifecycle is unregistered; Better Auth owns end-user credentials. Support/feedback links use the monitored `info@libraries.global` mailbox. These mailto links do not automatically create a support ticket or send an acknowledgment.

## Configure and operate

Keep SMTP credentials private in both apps. Strapi needs `CRON_ENABLED=true`, stable `EMAIL_LINK_SECRET` (at least 32 characters), a reachable `APP_INTERNAL_URL` for verified recipient lookup, the matching `STRAPI_BRIDGE_SECRET`, and the production `APP_PUBLIC_URL`. Set `EMAIL_DIGEST_ENABLED=true` and `EMAIL_REPORT_ENABLED=true` after rollout checks. Individual opt-ins still govern every weekly recipient. `EMAIL_SEND_INTERVAL_MS=1000` spaces live sends; Mailtrap sandbox uses 15000.

From the repository root:

```bash
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check status
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check previewReport <better-auth-user-id>
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check follows
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check digest
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check report
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check announcements
pnpm --filter @repo/strapi notifications:check deliver
pnpm --filter @repo/ui email:check
pnpm --filter @repo/ui email:check --to info@libraries.global
```

`status` and `previewReport` are read-only. Other operations run the real jobs and can queue or send messages to eligible users. Manual weekly runs use the last completed UTC week, even on Sunday. They cannot force a quiet week to send. The operations route requires the private service secret and is never exposed by a Next.js browser proxy.

Create announcements in Strapi Content Manager. Draft first; select the appropriate channel; use an internal relative path for the action link. Publication queues a single copy per existing profile within five minutes. Re-running the job or editing the same announcement does not resend it. Unpublishing before delivery cancels unsent email, but does not retract inbox copies already created. Essential announcements should concern service/security/legal/access. Product promotion belongs to the opt-in channel. The job scans the latest 100 published announcements.

Delivery runs each minute, up to 50 queued emails per pass, with a shared database lock, five attempts and exponential retry. In Notification records, inspect `emailState`, `attempts`, `sentAt`, `nextAttemptAt` and `lastError`. After correcting a provider problem, reset a failed record to `pending`, attempts to 0, and its next-attempt time to now. SMTP acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. A crash after SMTP accepts a message but before its sent state is saved can cause a duplicate.

App notifications are purged after 90 days by the daily 03:30 UTC job. Account deletion clears local notifications, subscriber attribution and queued mail sooner. Provider logs and manually imported campaign contacts require separate provider retention/erasure handling.

## Verification and remaining gaps

Regression tests cover every live preference on/off, independent weekly/product consent, quiet weeks, weekly boundaries, public-only report content, field-batch deduplication, repeat delivery, retries, deleted accounts, read ownership, operator authentication, newsletter confirmation/unsubscribe, announcements, retention, badge awards, staff permissions, role expiry, moderation, drive closure and event-feed failures. UI checks cover one shared observer, silent initial load, gesture-gated new-entry sound, sound-off behavior, profile navigation and email preference actions.

Remaining feature gaps are not exposed as pretend settings: submission reply threads, event reminders, followed-contributor posts, library notice-expiry reminders, staff operations summaries, separate tier-change alerts, account security-change notifications and deletion receipts. Pro instant library email alerts remain planned. Billing receipts depend on a future payment integration. Staff membership/permissions, role application decisions and role expiry are covered now. Manual role-grant creation/revocation has no separate receipt. Missing role-expiry notices during a CMS outage longer than 48 hours need a later recovery strategy.

Before broad email marketing, implement authenticated Brevo bounce/complaint/unsubscribe webhooks and contact sync, including provider-side erasure. Until then export only active confirmed newsletter subscribers, preserve Brevo suppression lists and reconcile campaign unsubscribes before another import. Do not re-import withdrawn contacts as subscribed. The app's optional category links remain independent; Brevo blocklisting may suppress a whole sender/channel. Check provider tracking settings before launch, especially for credential emails. Provider delivery logs and tracking redirects are provider-controlled; the application does not report opens/clicks or claim those settings have been disabled.

Local CMS legal and Knowledge content was updated through its MCP server: terms, privacy, cookies, accessibility, account/profile guide, review guide, badges, plans and account deletion. These database edits are local content; export/transfer reviewed documents as part of the content deployment. The update script makes a private backup and only publishes a revised entry if that entry already had a published version. Both weekly runtime flags are enabled locally. Brevo accepted the two labeled weekly delivery samples through the actual queue/worker at the approved info address, with zero retries; the test account and its records were deleted afterward. The focused verification passed 228 backend and 15 UI tests, both app typechecks and desktop/mobile browser checks. A read-only count after cleanup found zero opted-in weekly recipients; no batch email run was needed.

Shared visual design is in `packages/shared-data/email.ts`: `emailTemplate` is the shell, `EmailSection` supports metric grids, reading/library rows and note panels, and `withEmailPreferences` supplies current category controls in the queue worker. Every renderer returns subject, text and HTML. Use structured section data rather than concatenated HTML, and keep contributor-provided text escaped. `email-samples.mjs` contains fictional review fixtures, not production metrics or credentials. The real sender source in this table remains authoritative for wording and action destinations.

After editing shared templates, run `pnpm --filter @repo/shared-data build`. After editing a local Strapi plugin, build that plugin (`pnpm build` from its directory), then restart Strapi; its runtime loads the plugin’s compiled output. UI templates load the rebuilt shared package. Update or add a matching sample whenever a template family changes.

The email shell uses inline styling, presentation tables, a 640px Outlook fallback, system fonts, explicit background/text colors, a hidden preheader and a text alternative. Rounded corners may become square in older clients; the layout and actions still work. No remote images, external fonts, JavaScript, tracking pixel or CSS-only essential content is required. The fallback action label omits a credential query string while retaining the full URL in the link and text alternative. Browser previews are not an Outlook/Gmail rendering certification: review the delivered samples in the mail clients your audience uses.

`EMAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS` is configured in **both** local envs with the approved address. Set the same public address in production envs and Brevo’s campaign sender details. `EMAIL_FROM`, `EMAIL_REPLY_TO` and `APP_PUBLIC_URL` remain server-only configuration; use the production URL in deployment. No postal placeholder is sent. The live optional queue footer has category-specific withdrawal, preferences, privacy and the postal address. Essential account/service messages do not pretend to offer an optional unsubscribe.

[RFC 8058](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8058) specifies the one-click POST headers used by the live queue; verify that your provider’s DKIM signature covers those headers. The visible link opens a read-only confirmation page. Sample controls deliberately open Settings instead of carrying real signed subscription tokens, and sample sends have no one-click headers. They must not be used to validate the production unsubscribe endpoint. Postal sender details and visible withdrawal controls also support campaign requirements such as the [FTC’s commercial-email guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business); review the laws applying to your audience when launching campaigns.

## Managed Payments membership notices

`billing_update` is an essential access-notice kind. The private billing sync endpoint persists its projection and enqueues a notice in one transaction, using `billing:<subscription>:<revision>` for deduplication. Older or duplicate revisions do not enqueue another notice. Billing writes no community roles, trust or staff assignments. Verified-recipient lookup and deleted-account handling use the existing delivery service.

The application sends membership-ready, cancellation/resumption, payment-recovery, plan/interval change and paid-access-ended notices. First delinquency is inbox-only: Stripe/Link owns payment recovery. Essential email notices use `transactional` and are independent of optional activity, newsletter and marketing consent. Buying membership does not opt anyone into marketing. The billing notice utility is `src/utils/billing-notice.ts`; current email fallback language is English.

Stripe Managed Payments owns financial receipts, invoices, refund/credit confirmations and subscription financial reminders. Configure the appropriate Dashboard email settings and verify Link's sender; do not duplicate these as app emails. Sandbox financial receipts are not sent automatically. App billing previews are fictional, use the existing HTML/text email shell, and appear as `billing-*` samples in `apps/ui/scripts/email-samples.mjs`. Offline preview generation sends no email.

See [billing setup, webhook events and acceptance](../ui/BILLING.md). The purchase flag does not disable projection recovery or essential delivery. A real sandbox monthly purchase and a paid monthly-to-yearly change have been verified through signed events and private Strapi projection.
