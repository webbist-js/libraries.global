## Stop future renewal

_In short: Organisers use Developers → Manage API billing._

An organiser signs in, opens Developers and chooses Manage API billing to cancel renewal in Stripe's billing page. You can also manage renewal through Link using your financial confirmation. You do not need to delete an account or contact us just to stop renewal. Check the effective date and keep the confirmation. Settled access normally continues to the end of the paid period.

## Overage when you cancel

_In short: Overage already used stays payable._

If overage was switched on, overage incurred before cancellation remains payable and appears on the final invoice. To stop further overage at once, switch overage off or lower the cap. Doing so does not cancel overage already incurred.

## Statutory cancellation rights

_In short: UK consumers normally have 14 days to cancel._

If you are a consumer in the UK buying online, the cancellation period normally ends 14 days after the day the contract is entered into. Tell us clearly before that period ends; no special form is required. Asking for access to start immediately does not remove this right, but a proportionate amount for the service already supplied may be deducted where the law allows. Other countries may give additional rights. Stopping renewal is separate from cancelling a purchase. [Confirm treatment of business customers with legal review.]

## Request a refund

_In short: Use the transaction support route on your Stripe confirmation._

Request a payment refund through Link's transaction support route shown on your financial confirmation, or tell us at info@libraries.global. Include the invoice reference, account email, purchase date and your request. Our refund policy is subject to [Link's refund policy](https://support.link.com/questions/sold-through-link-refunds); Link's policy prevails if ours is more restrictive, and statutory rights are unaffected. This is not a promise of an unconditional refund for every purchase.

## Refund timing and method

_In short: Statutory refunds follow the legal deadline and original payment method._

For a valid UK statutory cancellation, reimbursement is due without undue delay and normally within 14 days of our being told, subject to any lawful deduction for service already supplied. The original payment method is used unless you agree otherwise, and no refund fee is charged. Your bank may take longer to show the funds. For other refund requests, Link's refund policy, the transaction terms and applicable law decide eligibility and timing.

## Work we never charge for

_In short: Failed or refused work is not billed._

We do not charge allowances, analysis credits or overage for failed, refused, timed-out or internally retried work. If an invoice includes such a charge, contact us and we will correct it. A failed renewal payment does not open new usage.

## What happens to access

_In short: A refunded or disputed current period ends paid access._

A full refund of the current paid period, or a payment dispute, ends API access for that period and stops its keys. Partial refunds and refunds of an earlier period do not remove a later settled period. Free accounts, contributions, recognition and verified staff tools are unaffected.

## Sponsorship agreements

_In short: Sponsorship follows its own agreement._

Sponsorship is a separate written agreement. Its term, cancellation and any refund are set out in that agreement, not in this policy.

## If something goes wrong

_In short: Check billing status before paying again._

If a payment is uncertain or access is still confirming, check the billing page and your financial confirmation before paying again. Contact info@libraries.global for access problems. For unresolved transaction issues, use Link's support process. You can read the [UK Consumer Contracts Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134). Your statutory remedies are unaffected.
