# Searching Libraries Global

Choose **Search Libraries Global** in the page toolbar, or press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux. Search opens over the page you are reading. Press **Escape** to close it and return to that page.

Type at least two characters. You can search for a library name, a place, a story or a guide. For example, try “Alexandria”, “opening hours” or “API”.

## Choose where to look

**All** groups results by type. The other buttons narrow the list:

- **Libraries and places** includes published library records, continents, countries, regions and local areas. Places can appear before any libraries have been recorded there.
- **Journal** searches published story titles, summaries and indexed metadata.
- **Knowledge** searches published guide titles, summaries and indexed metadata.
- **Events** shows reviewed upcoming or ongoing events from connected library feeds.

Site destinations such as the Atlas, Community and plans also appear in All. Search does not cover private profiles, account settings, pending contributions or unapproved discussions. Article body text and source citations are not searched yet.

The interface language selects the search language using the platform’s supported content fallback. Use the language controls in Journal or Knowledge to browse other available article languages.

## Open a result

Click a result, or use **↑** and **↓** to select one and **Enter** to open it. A library opens its record. A place opens its location page; local areas open Find libraries with that area selected. Journal and Knowledge results open the article. Events open their event page.

Counts marked **≈** are estimates. Search shows a short list from each group. Open Find libraries, Journal, Knowledge or Events to browse the full collection and use its filters.

## Recent searches

When you open a result, your last five search terms are saved in this browser. They appear when you open search with an empty field. Choose **Clear recent searches** to remove them. They are not saved to your profile or shared across devices. Browser storage can be disabled without preventing search.

## When something is missing

Try a shorter name or another spelling, or choose All to widen the search. A library that is missing from the index can be added through Contribute. A reviewer checks new records before they appear.

An empty Events tab can mean that no reviewed upcoming events have been published. A message about unavailable results means a source could not load. Results from other sources can still appear. If the whole search request fails, choose Retry.
